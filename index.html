📖 How to Play
Everything you need to know about Chicken Dinner
⚽

Making picks

Every match gives you three choices: Home Win, Draw, or Away Win. Tap the one you think will happen — you can pick as many or as few matches each gameweek as you like.

🔒

When picks lock

Each match locks the moment it kicks off — not the whole gameweek at once. So you can still change your mind on Sunday's match even after Friday's has already started.

🏆

Scoring

Get a pick right and you score 1 point. Results are added once matches finish, and your points update automatically on the Leaderboard and your Profile.

⚡

The Booster

Once per gameweek, you can boost one of your picks. If it's correct, it's worth 2 points instead of 1. Pick a match first, then tap "Use Booster" on it. Only one active at a time.

🍗

Chicken Dinner Boost

A once-per-season power move: doubles the points for every match in one gameweek of your choice. Activate it from your Profile before that gameweek's first match kicks off — you can't take it back after, and it can only ever be used once all season. It also stacks with your normal weekly Booster, so a boosted correct pick in your Chicken Dinner gameweek is worth 4 points instead of 1.

🎯

Over/Under 2.5 Goals

From Gameweek 2 onward, once you've made your Home/Draw/Away pick on a match, you can also predict whether the total goals scored will be Over or Under 2.5. Get it right for +1 bonus point — and your weekly Booster or Chicken Dinner Boost will double this too, same as your main pick.

👥

Leagues

Create a private league from the Leagues tab and share the invite code with friends, or join one using a code someone sends you. Each league shows its own mini-leaderboard of just its members.

🌍

Global Leaderboard

Separate from leagues, there's one global leaderboard ranking every player in Chicken Dinner by total points — see how you stack up against everyone, not just your friends.

👤

Team name

Head to your Profile to set a custom team name. It replaces the default name (taken from your email) everywhere — leaderboards, leagues, and the header.

📊

Accuracy

Your Profile also shows what % of your settled picks have been correct — a good way to see if your gut instinct is actually paying off.
